Pets

About Pets

Pets is a free set of care guides for the pets most often kept at home: dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, budgerigars, cockatiels and aquarium fish. Each guide covers the right home, food, a daily routine, handling and safety, with notes for the Indian climate.

Where the content comes from

All guides are written by us, in our own words, from widely accepted good practice in pet care. We do not copy text from books, websites or product leaflets. Written in November 2026.

What these guides are not

They are not veterinary advice. We do not describe illnesses, treatments, medicines or doses, and nothing here can replace an examination by a qualified veterinarian. Vaccination, deworming, neutering and diet for a sick, very young, pregnant or old animal are for your vet to decide.

Rules and laws

Notes about Indian rules (protected wildlife, registration of dogs, housing societies) are a general guide as understood in November 2026. Rules differ between states and cities and they change. Check with your local authority or the Forest Department before you act.
