Which pet suits you?

Answer six questions honestly. You will see which pets fit your home and routine, and why.

How to read the result

The guide compares your answers with what each pet needs in time, space, cost and company. It is a starting point for a family discussion, not a verdict. Read the full care guide of any pet you are considering, and if you can, spend time with one before deciding.

Three honest rules

  • If the time is not there, choose a pet that needs less of it, or wait.
  • A pet is never a surprise gift. The person who will care for it must choose it.
  • A child can help, but an adult is always the one responsible.
